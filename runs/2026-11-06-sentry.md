## What ran

- codeowners-comparison at `96f67d7` (`bench.py` with `--gh-codeowners-wasm`).
- gh-codeowners at `c1eaf48`, built with `cargo build --release`.
- gh-codeowners' WebAssembly build from `wasm/` at the same commit, built with `cargo build --release --target wasm32-unknown-unknown` (`opt-level = "s"`, 993,288 bytes). It runs in Node through [`node/wasm.js`](../node/wasm.js), so its time includes starting Node and `git ls-files -z`.

- Sentry at `063a6cb`, checked out with empty files: 773 rules, 22,256 files.

Times are medians of 11 runs; tools taking over 10 s ran once.
